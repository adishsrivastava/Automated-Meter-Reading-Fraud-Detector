Automated Meter Reading (AMR) & Utility Fraud Detector

Interactive AI Workbench: Test custom meter readings, physical tampering, and 24-hour load curves in real time.
How This Project Works (Plain-English Overview): Our AI catches electricity theft using two branches: 1. The "Eyes" (YOLOv8 + U-Net + ResNet-34) read the 7-digit LCD screen, wipe off flashlight glare, and check for magnets or broken security seals. 2. The "Brain" (LSTM + Variational Autoencoder) analyzes the 24-hour electricity usage curve to catch sudden flatlines or wire bypasses.
